Welcome to YBI

A place to know the Word and live it.

✦

Youth Bible Institute (YBI) is a Bible-learning ministry based in Marsabit, Kenya, serving young people through intentional Christian teaching, fellowship and spiritual formation.

YBI operates under Ephatha Mission of Africa, seeking to nurture a generation that knows Scripture, follows Christ and is prepared to serve others.
