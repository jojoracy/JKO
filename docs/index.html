Who we are

Faith. Identity. Purpose.

✦

Youth Bible Institute (YBI) is a holiday programme for high school and college students that helps Christian youth to be strongly founded in their faith and know and embrace their identity and values as Christians in a fast-changing world.

YBI encourages youth to understand their purpose and know that their existence is part of a bigger plan of God. It seeks to involve youth as co-creators and collaborators in the divine work of the church, especially in the expansion of the Gospel in Marsabit.

YBI is a long-term investment in the future shape of Christianity in Marsabit, encouraging Christian youth to strengthen their identity as a new generation of Christians, deepen their faith and claim their society for Christ.

Our mission

Bringing the Good News and building Christian foundations.

The mission of Ephatha Mission of Africa (EMA) is to bring the Good News of Jesus Christ to the continent of Africa and beyond through training pastors and church leaders to effectively minister to their congregations and evangelize.

In addition, EMA seeks to empower Christian youth by providing a much-needed spiritual support base, sound Biblical foundation and establishing Christian values in the lives of young people in Northern Kenya.

Who stands behind YBI

Ephatha Mission of Africa

Ephatha Mission of Africa is a registered Evangelical Church Organization headquartered at Ephatha Praise Mission, USA.

EMA has been operating in Africa for over 20 years, working with other related ministries in Kenya, Uganda and Ethiopia.

Mission prospect

What YBI seeks to cultivate.

The brochure describes YBI as a space where young people can grow through Scripture, Christian community, worship and authentic examples of faith.

01

Biblical discernment

Helping students effectively filter life through Biblical principles and providing Biblical teaching that clarifies boundaries for behaviour and lifestyle.

02

Personal faith

Instilling the discipline of studying the Word of God and giving youth the ability to experience God on a personal level.

03

Christian community

Creating a loving, all-inclusive atmosphere where Christian youth can develop their individual and spiritual lives.

04

Authentic models

Encouraging interaction with adults who authentically model the Christian faith.

05

Worship & connection

Providing a variety of worship experiences to help youth connect with God and with one another.

06

Spiritual development

Taking responsibility to influence the spiritual development of youth through an in-depth understanding of the Word.
